Burrow in a browser

Burrow's own Rust (its model, view-models and Markdown renderer) compiled to WebAssembly. Invented sample data, kept in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. On a phone it becomes a phone app.

Your own posts on this blyg, through your Studio session. Edits are kept in this browser at once and reach the blyg after you pause; offline, they wait. Publishing happens only when you choose it.
